# Third-party data notices

Raw source databases and downloaded publication supplements are not included.

## AADR

The Allen Ancient DNA Resource v66.p1 is the inferential core. The Harvard
Dataverse record declares CC0 1.0. Cite DOI 10.7910/DVN/FFIDCW and the AADR
data descriptor. This repository redistributes only aggregate derived outputs;
person-level AADR catalogues are intentionally excluded.

## AmtDB

AmtDB v1.009 is used for a marker-specific mitochondrial coverage and crosswalk
audit. The AmtDB site identifies the resource as CC BY 4.0. Cite DOI
10.1093/nar/gky843, attribute the database authors, and state that identifiers
were normalized and records were crosswalked.

## aYChr-DB

aYChr-DB v5.00 is used for a Y-chromosome coverage and crosswalk audit. Cite
DOI 10.1093/nargab/lqaa081. The article and supplement are CC BY 4.0; the
upstream GitHub repository did not display a repository-level license during
the 2026-08-03 audit. The raw workbook is therefore not redistributed.

## Post-freeze studies

Koken individual-level supplementary data are archived in Dryad under DOI
10.5061/dryad.4xgxd25nx and marked CC0. Boz-Barmak is cited from the final
Scientific Reports article under DOI 10.1038/s41598-026-62891-8.

Four Ulytau/Golden Horde rows were originally audited from a restricted
bioRxiv supplement and are deliberately absent from this repository. Only a
citation-level count linked to the final PNAS article, DOI
10.1073/pnas.2531003123, is retained. Do not publish those person-level rows
without verified final-supplement reuse terms or author permission.

The publication-linked Poseidon package must receive a package-specific
license and immutable commit check before version 1.0.
