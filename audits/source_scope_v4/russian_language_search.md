# C11 — Russian-language evidence search and inclusion register

Audit date: **2026-08-21**

## Search scope and wording

This was a structured supplementary search for Russian-language primary and
legacy evidence, not a PRISMA-complete database review. It covered records
about human archaeological remains recovered in Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan,
Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, or Turkmenistan that reported mtDNA, Y-chromosome, or
genome-wide data from which uniparental calls could be extracted.

Queries used for discovery and exact-title verification were:

```text
R1: ("древняя ДНК" OR палеогенетик* OR археогенетик*) AND
    (Казахстан OR Кыргызстан OR Киргиз* OR Узбекистан OR Таджикистан OR
     Туркменистан OR "Центральная Азия" OR "Средняя Азия")

R2: (митохондриальн* OR мтДНК OR "Y-хромосом*" OR гаплогрупп*) AND
    (древн* OR археолог* OR погребен* OR могильник OR курган) AND
    (Казахстан OR Кыргызстан OR Киргиз* OR Узбекистан OR Таджикистан OR
     Туркменистан OR БМАК OR Бактрийско-Маргиан* OR Андронов* OR сак* OR
     скиф* OR усун* OR кангюй* OR хунн* OR тюрк* OR "Золотая Орда")
```

Discovery was run against indexed eLIBRARY/EDN pages, CyberLeninka, RCSI
journal pages, and national/institutional repositories, followed by exact-title
and citation chasing. eLIBRARY did not provide a stable public export or a
defensible total hit count in this environment; therefore no numerical search
count or claim of exhaustive coverage is made. Exact articles were checked
against journal/repository pages or full text where available.

Inclusion rule: human archaeological remains from one of the five republics
plus a reported ancient mtDNA/Y result or reusable genome-wide data. Excluded:
modern-only genetics; animal/pathogen-only studies without reusable human host
calls; archaeology/palaeoanthropology without DNA; news and popular summaries;
and samples recovered outside the five republics.

## Reference-ready records

### 1. Botai, Kazakhstan — include, legacy tier

**Джансугурова Л.Б., Зайберт В.Ф., Китов Е.П., Иксан О.А., Нуржибек,
Жунусова Г.С., Джантаева К.Б., Кузовлева Е.Б., Хусаинова Э.М.** (2017).
Палеогенетическое исследование человеческих останков энеолитического периода
с поселения Ботай. *Известия Национальной академии наук Республики Казахстан.
Серия биологическая и медицинская*, **4(322)**, 78–88.
<https://archeo-lib.kz/assets/%D0%94%D0%9D%D0%9A_%D0%91%D0%BE%D1%82%D0%B0%D0%B9.pdf>

- DOI: none found after exact-title/issue checks.
- Eligibility: one Eneolithic human from Botai; mtDNA and Y-STR-based result.
- Use: cite in the regional/legacy layer, not as an independent additional
  individual when the same Botai person is already represented.
- Critical qualification: the paper's Y-O2 prediction is superseded by the
  later sequence-based R-L1432/R1b1a1a1a call in de Barros Damgaard et al.
  (2018). Do not pool the old Y-STR prediction with sequence-based Y calls.
- Author-list note: some institutional bibliographies insert B.O. Bekmanov and
  others omit him. The author list above follows the article citation used by
  the existing verified bibliography and independent author profiles; inspect
  the title page once more at typesetting if the journal requires every author.

### 2. Berel, Kazakhstan — include, regional-language companion/duplicate

**Джансугурова Л.Б., Мусралина Л.З., Хусаинова Е.М., Бекманов Б.О.,
Самашев З.С., Гнекки-Русконе Г.А., Краузе Й.** (2020).
Палеогенетический анализ костных останков людей, представляющих
хунно-сяньбийский пласт Береля Казахстанской части Алтая. *Мир Большого
Алтая*, **6(2)**, 931–940.
<https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/paleogeneticheskiy-analiz-kostnyh-ostankov-lyudey-predstav-lyayuschih-hunno-syanbiyskiy-plast-berelya-kazahstanskoy-chasti-altaya>

- DOI: none found in the full text or exact-title metadata.
- Eligibility: 14 Berel individuals; 1,237,207-SNP capture, mtDNA and Y calls.
- Use: add as the Russian-language regional report and archaeological context.
  Its 14 individuals correspond to the same Berel series subsequently
  represented in Gnecchi-Ruscone et al. (2021); do **not** add 14 new
  persons or count this as an independent dataset.

### 3. Tutkaul, Tajikistan / Kaylu, Turkmenistan — include, with site-specific qualification

**Шнайдер С.В., Березина Н.Я., Филимонова Т.Г., Алишер кызы С.,
Бужилова А.П.** (2023). Результаты нового изучения антропологических
материалов Центральной Азии (по материалам стоянок Туткаул и Кайлю).
*Российская археология*, **4**, 7–19.
<https://doi.org/10.31857/S0869606323040165>;
<https://journals.rcsi.science/0869-6063/article/view/232620>;
EDN: <https://elibrary.ru/HEHBNP>.

- Eligibility: the article reports a mitochondrial genome and Y-chromosome
  analysis for Tutkaul burial 2 in Tajikistan.
- Deduplication: this is the same Tutkaul individual already represented
  through Posth et al. (2023); cite it for regional-language/contextual
  coverage, not as a new person.
- The Kaylu individuals from Turkmenistan were sexed by enamel-peptide analysis
  and radiocarbon dated, but the article does not report their mtDNA or Y calls.
  Kaylu therefore does not add a Turkmenistan genetic record to the review.

## Conditional grey/legacy record

**Джансугурова Л.Б., Байтанаев Б.А., Хусаинова Е.М., Нуржибек,
Жунусова Г.С., Иксан О.А., Джантаева К.Б., Китов Е.П.** (2016). Анализ
митохондриальной ДНК находки «Урджарская принцесса». In: *Культурное
наследие Евразии (с древности до наших дней): сборник научных статей*.
Алматы: Институт археологии им. А.Х. Маргулана, 698–711.

- DOI: none found.
- Citation verification: the full citation is reproduced in institutional
  bibliographies and in a later peer-reviewed Urzhar archaeology paper:
  <https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/kurgan-urdzhar-istoriya-otkrytiya-i-nekotorye-itogi-issledovaniya>.
- The original chapter/full text was not independently located during this
  audit. Keep it in the supplementary legacy-search register; do not import a
  haplogroup into the quantitative catalogue solely from secondary reporting.

## Explicit exclusions and country-level outcome

| Geography | Outcome of this supplementary search | Reason/handling |
|---|---|---|
| Kazakhstan | Botai 2017 and Berel 2020 reference-ready; Urzhar 2016 conditional | Botai and Berel are duplicates/companions of later sequence datasets; Urzhar full text not recovered. |
| Tajikistan | Tutkaul/Kaylu 2023 reference-ready | Same Tutkaul person as in the broader 2023 palaeogenomic publication. |
| Kyrgyzstan | No additional eligible Russian-language primary article verified | Russian news on Boz-Barmak is secondary to the final English-language paper and was excluded. |
| Uzbekistan | No additional eligible Russian-language primary ancient-human-DNA article verified | News and popular summaries of published English-language studies were excluded as duplicates/secondary evidence. |
| Turkmenistan | No additional genetic record from a Russian-language article verified | Kaylu in Shnaider et al. has peptide sex/radiocarbon data but no mtDNA/Y call; archaeology/palaeoanthropology-only records were excluded. |

Also excluded was the 2017 Russian-language paper on human remains of the
Hunnic period because its analysed ancient remains are from Hungary, not from
one of the five target republics. Modern-population studies and Russian-language
news releases were not counted as ancient-person evidence.

## Safe manuscript wording

> A structured supplementary Russian-language search was conducted in
> eLIBRARY/EDN-indexed records, CyberLeninka, RCSI journal pages, and relevant
> institutional repositories using Russian ancient-DNA, palaeogenetic,
> mitochondrial, and Y-chromosome terms combined with the names of the five
> Central Asian republics and major archaeological entities. Three
> reference-ready regional records were verified (Botai, Berel, and Tutkaul),
> all of which overlap or are superseded by later sequence-based person-level
> datasets. One Urzhar chapter was retained as conditional grey/legacy evidence
> because its citation, but not its original full text, could be independently
> verified. This supplementary search was not a PRISMA-complete eLIBRARY export,
> and no claim of exhaustive Russian-language coverage is made.
